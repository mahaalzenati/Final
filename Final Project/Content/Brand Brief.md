# 📘 StockPilot – Brand Brief

## 1. Target Audience

Owners and managers of small and medium-sized retail stores, as well as individuals responsible for inventory management and replenishment decisions.

## 2. Content Goals

### 01. Brand Awareness
Introduce StockPilot to store owners, explain the inventory management problems it addresses, and show how it can help businesses manage their inventory more effectively.

### 02. Education
Educate the audience about common inventory management challenges, such as:
- Unexpected stockouts.
- Excess inventory and overstocking.
- Poor replenishment decisions.
- Delayed responses to changing demand.

### 03. Product Awareness
Highlight StockPilot's key features and practical value, including:
- Inventory monitoring.
- Sales analysis.
- Telegram notifications.
- Cost and risk analysis.
- AI-powered replenishment recommendations.

### 04. Conversion
Encourage store owners to explore StockPilot, try the system, and get in touch to learn more about its capabilities.

## 3. Tone of Voice

**Confident, professional, and approachable.**

- **Professional:** Builds trust and presents StockPilot as a reliable inventory management solution.
- **Simple:** Explains features clearly without unnecessary technical terminology.
- **Persuasive:** Focuses on real problems, practical solutions, and business value.
- **Practical:** Addresses everyday challenges that store owners can relate to.
- **Confident:** Communicates the product's value without exaggeration or unrealistic promises.

## 4. Visual Identity

### Brand Colors

| Color | Hex Code |
|---|---|
| Navy | `#102A43` |
| Turquoise | `#61DCC5` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

### Visual Style: Modern Minimal Design

- Clean, modern, and simple layouts.
- Balanced spacing and clear visual hierarchy.
- Avoid overcrowded designs.
- Maintain consistency with the brand color palette.
- Ensure strong contrast and readable typography.

## 5. Brand Communication Principles

- Focus on the real needs of store owners.
- Connect product features to practical business challenges.
- Use clear, direct, and accessible language.
- Avoid exaggerated claims about the system's capabilities.
- Maintain consistency across visual and verbal communication.
